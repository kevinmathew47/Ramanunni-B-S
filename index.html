About

Profile / 01

A cinematographer who studied philosophy first — and still frames every shot as a question about how we see.

Ramanunni B S is a cinematographer, director, editor and creative director from Trivandrum, Kerala. After a BA in Philosophy at University College, Trivandrum, he trained in cinematography at the K. R. Narayanan National Institute of Visual Science & Arts, where he also trained as an editor. Over a decade he has shot features, short films, documentaries, music videos and commercial campaigns, with work selected at festivals across India and abroad. He is currently a Video Production Specialist at Ramee Dream Hotel, Dubai.
